About the Founder

Lars Ender – Founder of ValueRadar

Lars Ender is the founder of ValueRadar, a platform designed to make fundamental stock analysis more transparent, understandable, and data-driven.

ValueRadar combines company metrics, quality criteria, and risk indicators to help long-term investors compare companies in a structured way. The platform focuses on substance: profitability, financial stability, growth, and transparent valuation.

The goal of ValueRadar is to turn complex financial information into a clearer analytical view for investors who want to understand the business behind the stock.
